# zeetab — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

zeetab does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any user data.

- **All data stays in your browser.** Your shortcuts, sections, and settings
  are stored locally using your browser's localStorage. They never leave
  your device and are not accessible to the developer.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.** zeetab has no server, no
  telemetry, and no third-party analytics of any kind.
- **The only network requests** are favicon lookups: for each shortcut you
  add, the icon may be fetched from `icons.duckduckgo.com` based on that
  site's domain. If you upload a custom icon instead, it is stored locally
  and no request is made.
- **Import/export** is a local file operation that you trigger explicitly.

If this policy ever changes, the change will be visible in this file's
public history: https://github.com/pZacca/zeetab/commits/main/PRIVACY.md

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/pZacca/zeetab/issues
